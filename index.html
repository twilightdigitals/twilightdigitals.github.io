Operation: CrossTalk

An asymmetric cooperative escape room for two players on two phones.

CO-OP ESCAPE 2 PLAYERS · 2 PHONES REALTIME WEBSOCKETS

⚠️ CRITICAL DIRECTIVE

Rule #1: NEVER peek at each other's screen! All intelligence must be communicated verbally out loud across the room.

One player holds the ticking bomb console with strikes and unstable modules. The other holds the classified Field Manual with cryptographic matrices. Neither player can survive alone.

🛠️ The Operative ("The Hands")

Sees the ticking countdown, strikes, cuts wires, tunes the oscilloscope, and enters memory sequences. Has no rules manual.

📖 The Analyst ("The Brains")

Holds the classified manual with wire logic matrices and frequency guidelines. Can advise, but cannot touch the device.
